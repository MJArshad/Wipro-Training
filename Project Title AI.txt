Project Title

AI-Powered Smart Complaint Analysis System

1. Problem Statement

Organizations receive hundreds or thousands of customer complaints through:

Email
Chat
Website forms
Customer support portals

Currently, support teams manually:

Read complaints
Identify categories
Determine urgency
Assign departments

This process is:

Time-consuming
Error-prone
Expensive
Difficult to scale
Example Complaints

Complaint 1:

Money deducted from my account but order is not confirmed.

Complaint 2:

My internet connection is down since morning.

Complaint 3:

I received a damaged product.

Different teams must handle each complaint.

2. Proposed Solution

Build an AI-based system that:

Accepts customer complaints.
Understands complaint context.
Categorizes complaint.
Detects urgency.
Generates summary.
Assigns department automatically.
Output Example

Input:

Money deducted but ticket was not booked.

Output:

Plain Text
1
Category: Payment Issue
2
Priority: High
3
Department: Finance Team
4
 
5
Summary:
6
Customer payment was deducted, but booking was not completed.

Traditional Coding Approach

Problem:

Customers write complaints differently.

Examples:

Plain Text
1
My card got charged twice.
2
 
3
The amount was deducted.
4
 
5
Payment completed but service unavailable.
6
 
7
Money got debited unexpectedly.
Show more lines

All mean similar things.

Rule-based systems struggle to identify these variations.

4. Why Use AI?

AI understands meaning instead of exact keywords.

Example:

Plain Text
1
Money deducted but service not received.
Show more lines

and

Plain Text
1
My account was charged and I got nothing.
Show more lines

Both express the same issue.

AI can:

✅ Understand context

✅ Handle multiple writing styles

✅ Summarize long complaints

✅ Detect customer sentiment

✅ Predict urgency

✅ Reduce manual work
