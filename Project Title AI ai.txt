Slide 3: Introduction
Project Overview

Organizations receive large numbers of customer complaints through:

Email
Support Portals
Chat Systems
Mobile Applications

Manually reviewing and routing complaints is slow and inefficient.

This project uses AI to automate complaint understanding and classification.
